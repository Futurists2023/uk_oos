# Cold Email Outreach Playbook — UK Retail Digital Shelf Watchdog

## Overview & Positioning
This playbook outlines the exact outbound messaging strategy for pitching Commercial Directors, Heads of Wholesale, and National Account Managers at UK SME & Challenger Brands (£2M–£25M turnover).

Instead of pitching a generic "analytics tool" or a feature list, the email delivers an **unforced revenue outage audit** in plain text directly in the body of the message.

---

## 1. The Winning First-Touch Email Template

• [Product 4]

Best,
[Your Name]

## 2. Core Psychological & Structural Rules

1. **Zero Links in Touch 1:**
   - Links in cold emails trigger corporate spam filters (Google Workspace / Microsoft 365).
   - Zero links guarantees 100% inbox deliverability and removes click suspicion.
2. **Front-Loaded Duration ("For 6 days..."):**
   - Front-loading the duration ensures the lock screen / mobile preview snippet shows accumulated neglect rather than repeating the subject line.
3. **Verified DTC Stock ("All 5 are in stock on your site today"):**
   - Proves this is an **unforced retailer replenishment failure**, not a factory or supply shortage.
   - Eliminates their excuses before they can make them.
4. **"Core Lines" vs. "Hero SKUs":**
   - Avoid agency jargon like "Hero SKUs". "Core lines" or "best-sellers" is natural British retail terminology.
5. **The 3-Day Threshold Filter:**
   - Promising to only alert when lines stay dark for **over 3 days** eliminates their fear of inbox spam from minor 24-hour warehouse glitches.
6. **Ultra-Concise CTA:**
   - Single-sentence action question (*"Should I alert you when a core line stays dark for over 3 days?"*).
   - Requires an effortless, one-word reply from the recipient: **"Yes."**

## 3. The Follow-Up (Email 2 — When They Reply "Yes")

Once the recipient replies positively, immediately deliver their live digital sheet link:

```text
Subject: Re: [Original Subject]

Hi [Name],

Great — I put together a live digital shelf sheet for [Brand Name] across Boots, Lookfantastic, and Holland & Barrett so you can see full range availability and outage durations:

https://[your-domain]/b/[brand-token]

I can set this to automatically notify you the moment a core line slips out of stock or when [Retailer] restocks. Which email addresses on your team should receive the alerts?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

---

## 4. Pre-Send Quality Checklist

Before sending any first-touch email, verify:
- [ ] **Exact Retailer Status:** Visually confirm the products are currently marked "Out of Stock" on the target retailer website.
- [ ] **DTC Availability Check:** Verify that the brand's own website has the products **In Stock** (to avoid alerting them on factory backorders).
- [ ] **Duration Verification:** Ensure the outage has lasted at least 3–4 days according to scraper timestamps.
- [ ] **Accurate Product & Size Naming:** Match the exact titles and sizes listed on the retailer's digital shelf.
- [ ] **Correct Recipient:** Target the Commercial Director, Head of Sales, or National Account Manager on LinkedIn.
